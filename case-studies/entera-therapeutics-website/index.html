01 · The briefLook as credible as the science behind it

ENTERA Therapeutics works in gastroenterology, hepatology and clinical nutrition. They develop products, run scientific collaborations and hold medical-education events. Their main audience is healthcare professionals, so the website had to look professional and trustworthy, and match their logo and brand from day one.

They also needed practical things: a clear way for doctors, partners and job seekers to get in touch, and a site the team could share on WhatsApp and LinkedIn with a proper preview. All of this without a monthly hosting bill.

02 · DesignBuilt from their logo

We took the colours, shapes and tone straight from ENTERA's logo: deep navy, teal and an orange accent for actions. We explored two design directions, a dark "luxury" style and a light corporate one. The client chose the light corporate version as the better fit for a medical audience.

  • A hero slider built around their "quest to heal" story.
  • A page for each speciality (Gastroenterology, Hepatology, Clinical Nutrition), each with an illustrated header, a sticky section menu and links to the others.
  • Products grouped by speciality, plus signature pillars for science, innovation and medical education.
  • An event page with a slider made from the event flyer, key themes, and a "Register interest" button that opens the enquiry form already filled in for that event.

03 · The buildA static site with no server to pay for

A company website mostly shows information. It doesn't need a database or a server running all day. So we built it as a static site (HTML, CSS and JavaScript) and hosted it on GitHub Pages with their own domain and HTTPS.

Free, fast hostingGitHub Pages serves the site over HTTPS at no monthly cost. The client only pays for the domain.
Nothing to patchNo server, plugins or database means fewer security updates and almost nothing that can break.
Proper link previewsOpen Graph tags and a branded share image, so links look right on WhatsApp, LinkedIn and Facebook.
Analytics readyGoogle Tag Manager tracks page views between sections and every enquiry that's sent.

04 · EnquiriesStraight into Google Sheets and the sales inbox

A static site can't store form submissions on its own, so we connected the enquiry form to Google Apps Script. When someone sends an enquiry:

  1. A new row is added to a Google Sheet the team already knows how to use: name, phone, email, enquiry type, message and date.
  2. An email alert goes to the sales team at the same moment.
  3. The visitor sees a clear confirmation message.

Visitors choose an enquiry type (product information, scientific collaboration, business partnership, event registration or careers), so each enquiry reaches the right person faster. A hidden spam trap keeps bots out of the sheet.

No paid form service, no CRM subscription and no database. Just tools the team already had, connected properly.

05 · The resultA site the client is proud to share

The client was very impressed with how closely the website matched their brand and expectations. They now have a professional online presence they can confidently share with doctors and partners, and a simple, reliable way to collect enquiries, all for the cost of a domain name.

06 · What we learnedChoose the simplest setup that does the job

  • Not every business needs a server. For a company website, static hosting is faster, safer and free.
  • Use the tools the team already knows. A Google Sheet is easier for a sales team than a new dashboard.
  • Design from the brand outward. Starting from the logo made the first draft feel like ENTERA, and kept rounds of changes short.
