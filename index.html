Our vision

Simplifying business.

Too many manufacturers and retailers run on spreadsheets, disconnected software and manual re-entry. Stock counts don't match the website, orders get missed, and month-end takes days.

We fix that with one connected system, designed around how your team already works. Less admin, fewer errors, and a clear view of your business at any moment.

FAQ

Questions businesses ask us.

What does Simplifex do?

Simplifex builds custom ERP systems and e-commerce websites for manufacturers, retailers and online brands, and connects them so stock, orders, invoices and accounts stay in sync in one system.

Where is Simplifex located?

Our office is in Perumbavoor, Ernakulam district, Kerala, India. We visit your factory, store or warehouse during discovery to map how work really happens.

Which ERP modules can you build?

Inventory and warehouse, production and MRP, sales and billing, purchasing, accounting and finance, HR and payroll, quality control, and reports and dashboards. You can start with the modules you need and add more as you grow.

Can you connect my online store to my inventory and accounts?

Yes. Every online or marketplace order reserves stock, raises a tax-ready invoice in accounts and books the courier automatically, so you never oversell or re-enter data.

Do I have to pay per-user licence fees?

No. You own the software and the code, so you can add staff and stores without paying more per seat.

Can you move data from our current software or spreadsheets?

Yes. Data migration from your old system, staff training and a smooth switchover are part of every go-live.
